How do beliefs propagate?

You will read a short story in which a group of people move an object around a room, exit the room one by one, and tell each other where they think the object is.

Next, you'll answer 12 questions about what each person believes, or what they believe others believe, including counterfactual scenarios about what would change if one event had gone differently.

The stories come from TemporalGraph-ToM, a benchmark of higher order theory-of-mind reasoning in LLMs. After the task, you can compare your answers with Fable 5.

This is a test version. Answers aren't saved.

How the stories work

1. Everyone in the room sees each move, except the person it is hidden from.

2. Once someone leaves the room, they see nothing more.

3. After everyone has left, some people make claims. A public claim reaches everyone; a private one reaches only the person told.

4. A listener believes a claim only if the speaker left the room later than the listener did.
